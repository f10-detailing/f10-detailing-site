Why workshops partner with us

You already have the customer. We sell the wait.

A car in for repair belongs to a customer who's already paying attention, and nobody else is selling to them in that window. F10 works it for you, as your marketing department rather than another vendor.

You fix, we detail

Our contracted detailers handle every job. Your technicians never touch a vacuum or a chemical, and repairs keep moving exactly as they do today.

The car never leaves your lot

No handover, no off-site liability, no transit risk on a customer's vehicle. Everything happens inside your own fence line.

The Fix & Shine system
