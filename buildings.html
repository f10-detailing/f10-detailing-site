Yearly laundry maintenance

One visit a year for your shared laundries.

Shared laundries get heavy use and little attention until something breaks or a resident complains. We come once a year, give the room a proper maintenance clean, and tell you what we found.

What the visit covers

  • Washers: drum, door seal, detergent drawer and exterior cleaned
  • Dryers: lint filters and accessible lint housing cleaned
  • Laundry tubs, taps and benches descaled and cleaned
  • Floors, skirting and behind-machine areas where accessible
  • A short condition report for the building manager

How the plan works

  • We inspect each laundry and quote per building
  • You approve once and we book the visit each year
  • We work at a time that suits residents
  • You get one report per visit for your records
  • Faults we find are flagged for your appliance technician
We're cleaners, not appliance or electrical technicians. We don't repair machines, open sealed panels or certify dryer exhaust ducts. If we spot a fault, we note it in the report so your service contractor can fix it.
How it works

From walk-through to scheduled visits.

  1. Walk-through

    We look at the carpets, furniture and laundries and agree what's in scope.

  2. Fixed quote

    One quote per building, so your committee or owner can approve it once.

  3. Scheduled work

    Visits booked to suit residents, done by the uniformed, insured F10 team.

  4. Sign-off

    You get a record of what was done and anything we noticed that needs attention.

Questions

Common questions.

Do you clean private homes?

No. F10 works on commercial accounts only: owners corporations, building and facility managers, property managers and furnished-apartment operators.

How long does carpet take to dry?

It depends on the carpet, airflow and weather. We extract as much water as possible and will tell you what to expect for your building before we start.

Can all stains be removed?

Many can, but some (for example bleach marks or permanent dyes) can't be lifted by any cleaning method. We'll tell you honestly before and after.

Are you insured?

Yes. Every F10 job is covered by $5M public liability insurance, and we can provide a certificate of currency for your records.

How is pricing worked out?

Every building is different, so we quote after a walk-through, based on area, number of items and access. There's no cost for the walk-through.
