What that means for you

What every client gets.

Vetted people

Everyone passes an interview and a hands-on skills assessment before they work on a client's car or in anyone's home.

One way of working

Every job follows the F10 checklist and workflow, including how customer belongings are bagged and returned in vehicles.

Supplied chemicals

F10 supplies the chemicals used on every job, so results and safety are consistent across crews.

Uniformed and identifiable

The F10 team wears uniform and ID on every job, so you always know who's at your door.

Ongoing quality checks

Random spot checks, client feedback and regular performance reviews, with retraining if something isn't up to standard.

Fully insured

$5M public liability on every job. Certificate of currency available on request.

Our rules on site

Your clients stay your clients.

Everyone on the F10 team agrees in writing never to solicit or take work directly from the workshops, homes or customers we serve. On workshop jobs, review invitations go to the workshop, not to us.
