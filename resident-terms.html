Resident Service Terms

These terms apply when you book a washing machine clean, dryer clean or laundry bundle with F10 Detailing for your home. Please read them before you book. Version 2026-10-18.

1. Who we are

"F10", "we" and "us" means F10 Detailing (ABN 54 457 527 202), phone 0420 264 998. "You" means the person making the booking. The work is carried out by trained F10 contractors who follow F10's procedures and are covered by F10's $5M public liability insurance.

2. What the service includes

  • Washing machine yearly clean: deep clean of the drum, door seal and detergent drawer, clearing the pump filter where it is accessible without tools that open the machine, and cleaning the outside of the machine.
  • Dryer yearly clean: cleaning the lint filter and the lint housing where accessible, the drum and door seal, and the outside of the machine.
  • Laundry bundle: both of the above in one visit.
  • If we notice a fault, wear or a safety concern, we'll tell you and note it on the job record.

3. What the service does not include

  • We are cleaners, not appliance technicians, plumbers or electricians. We don't repair machines, replace parts, open sealed or electrical panels, or disconnect water, drainage or power connections.
  • We don't clean or certify dryer exhaust ducts inside walls or ceilings, and we don't test electrical safety or confirm that a machine is safe to use.
  • We don't move machines that are stacked, built in, bolted down, or that can't be moved safely by one person. We may slide a free-standing machine a short distance to reach it, and put it back where it was.
  • Cleaning can't fix mechanical faults, worn parts, persistent smells caused by a fault, or permanent stains and rust.

4. Booking and payment

  • Bookings are made through our booking page during your building's service week. Prices are shown on the booking page. F10 is not currently registered for GST, so no GST is added to your price.
  • For an available time, you pay online when you book. Your booking is confirmed once payment is received. An unpaid booking is released after 30 minutes.
  • If you ask for a time that is already taken or unavailable, you also choose a second, available time. You won't be charged until we confirm. We'll try to add a worker for your first choice; if we can't, we'll book your second choice and let you know.
  • Payments are processed by Stripe. We don't see or store your card details.

5. Access to your home

  • By booking, you permit F10 and its contractor to enter your home at the booked time, only to reach the laundry area and carry out the service.
  • Someone aged 18 or over should be home to let us in. If no one will be home, you can arrange access (for example a key with the building manager or concierge) and describe it in your access notes. By doing so you authorise us to enter using that access, and you're responsible for having arranged it.
  • Our contractors wear F10 uniform and identification. They won't go into other rooms unless you ask them to, and won't move or handle your belongings beyond what's needed to reach the machine.
  • We'll call your mobile, then your backup number, if we can't get in. If we can't get access within 15 minutes of the start of your time window, the visit counts as missed (see section 8).

6. Before we arrive

  • Take clothes and items out of the machines, and clear a path to the laundry and the space directly in front of the machines.
  • Please keep pets in another room while we work.
  • Tell us in your access notes about any known faults, leaks, recent repairs, or anything unusual about your machines.
  • Water taps and hoses should be in normal working order. If we see a damaged hose, leaking tap or other risk, we may decide not to proceed with that machine until it's fixed.

7. Machine condition, damage and liability

  • Your rights under Australian Consumer Law. Our services come with guarantees that cannot be excluded under the Australian Consumer Law. Nothing in these terms limits those rights.
  • If we cause damage, we'll take responsibility for loss or damage caused by our contractor's negligence or failure to provide the service with due care and skill, and we'll fix the problem or pay the reasonable cost of fixing it.
  • What we are not responsible for, to the extent the law allows: faults or wear that existed before our visit; failures caused by the age, condition or design of a machine; faults in water, drainage, power or venting connections that we didn't touch; damage caused by information you didn't give us (for example a known fault); and loss that isn't a reasonably foreseeable result of our work.
  • Older machines. Seals, hoses, filters and other parts on older machines can be brittle. We take care, but a part that is already near the end of its life may fail when it's handled or cleaned. Where that happens and we haven't been negligent, the cost of the part or repair is yours.
  • Photos. Our contractor may take photos of the machines before and after the clean, as a record of their condition. We only photograph the machines and the laundry area.
  • Report problems quickly. If you think something has gone wrong because of our visit, please call us within 48 hours with photos if you can, and stop using the machine if it's leaking or unsafe. This helps us look into it while the details are fresh. It doesn't take away any rights you have under the Australian Consumer Law.

8. Changes, cancellations and missed visits

  • You can change or cancel your booking for free up to 24 hours before your time window starts. Cancellations made in time are refunded in full.
  • If you cancel less than 24 hours before, or we can't get access at the booked time, we'll offer you one free move to another available time in the same or a future service week. If that second visit is also cancelled late or missed, the payment isn't refunded.
  • If we have to cancel or can't finish the job for a reason on our side, we'll rebook you at no cost or give you a full refund, whichever you prefer.
  • If we decide not to proceed with a machine for safety reasons (section 6), we'll refund the price of that machine's clean or rebook it once the issue is fixed.

9. Safety

Our contractor may pause or stop work if they believe the work area is unsafe, or if they're treated in a threatening or abusive way. Where work stops for these reasons, we'll discuss rebooking with you.

10. Your personal information

We collect your name, unit, phone numbers, email and access notes only to manage your booking, contact you about it, and keep records of the work. We share them only with the contractor doing your job and with service providers we use to run bookings and payments (such as Stripe for payments, and Google, where our booking records are stored). We don't sell your information. You can ask us what we hold about you, or ask us to correct it, by calling 0420 264 998.

11. Complaints

If you're unhappy with any part of the service, call us on 0420 264 998 and we'll work with you to put it right. If we can't resolve it, you can contact Consumer Affairs Victoria.

12. General

These terms are governed by the laws of Victoria, Australia. If any part of these terms can't be enforced, the rest still applies. We may update these terms for future bookings; the version you agreed to when you booked applies to that booking.
