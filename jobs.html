The deal

What you get, and what you bring.

F10 provides

  • Consistent work from our partner workshops and buildings
  • Marketing and finding customers, handled for you
  • Training in the F10 system
  • Chemicals and consumables
  • Uniform, ID and branded materials
  • Scheduling, support and quality checks

You bring

  • An ABN (you'll work as an independent contractor)
  • Your own tools: vacuum, spot cleaner, brushes, towels
  • Attention to detail and respect for people's property
  • Reliability: on time, every time
  • Willingness to follow a proven system
  • Clear, professional communication
Room to grow: senior contractor rate, team leader, trainer and manager roles, and partnership opportunities in new suburbs for people who consistently do excellent work.
How hiring works

  1. Apply

  2. Meet & greet

  4. Train & start

    Sign the contractor agreement, learn the F10 system, and do your first job with a check at the end.
