Questions

Common questions

How is the price worked out?

Residential jobs depend on the size of the home and what you need, so we give you a free quote first, usually after a quick chat and a few photos. Appliance upkeep has fixed prices.

Do I need to be home?

It's best if you're home at the start and for the final walk-through. If you can't be, tell us how we'll get in and we'll confirm it with you before the day.

Do you bring your own products and equipment?

Yes. The F10 team brings the equipment and F10-supplied products. If you'd like us to use a particular product in your home, just let us know.

Will an end of lease clean get my bond back?

We clean thoroughly to a standard checklist, but the bond decision is your agent's or landlord's. If they raise anything we missed from what we agreed, call us.

Are you insured?

Yes. Every F10 job is covered by $5M public liability insurance.
