The Fix & Shine system

Four touchpoints that sell it before anyone asks.

Customers don't walk in planning to buy detailing. We design, print and place four pieces of signage, free, that take them from drop-off to "yes".

  1. Drop-off poster

    Reframes the wait itself: no extra time, no extra trip.

  2. Floor & door graphic

    Points the way to enquire and signals a real, in-demand service.

  3. Front desk menu

    Clear tiers that make the decision easy for an already-stressed customer.

    A small reward that turns "yes" into a win, not a second-guessed spend.
